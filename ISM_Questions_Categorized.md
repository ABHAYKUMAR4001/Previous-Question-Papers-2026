## 1. Hypothesis Testing (Z-test, t-test, Paired t-test)

### Q1 [2022-23 EC3 Makeup, 5M]

### Q2 [2022-23 EC3 Makeup, 5M]

### Q4 [2023-24 EC3 Makeup, 7M]

### Q5 [2023-24 EC3 Regular, 7M]

### Q6 [Sept 2024 EC3R, 5M]
